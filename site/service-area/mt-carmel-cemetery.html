Grave Cleaning & Decorating at Mt Carmel Cemetery (Wyandotte, MI)

Yes — we clean and decorate graves at Mt Carmel Cemetery in Wyandotte, including headstone cleaning and seasonal flowers that follow the cemetery's official decoration policy.

Mt Carmel Cemetery, part of Our Lady of the Scapular parish, is managed by Catholic Funeral & Cemetery Services (CFCS) of the Archdiocese of Detroit and has its own written rules — including approved container sizes, specific windows for holiday decorations, and restrictions on plantings and permanent items. We check these rules before every visit so nothing you place gets removed.

Our service at Mt Carmel — Cemetery-Compliant Light Cleaning + Approved Flower Holder Refresh ($75–$110 per visit; 2–3-visit plans $150–$200/year):

  • Gentle headstone cleaning with soft brushes and pH-neutral, stone-safe cleaners — never bleach or pressure washers
  • Gentle grass trimming around the base
  • Fresh flowers in approved containers only (max 12" tall, max 8" opening, up to 2 per grave)
  • Photos sent to you after every visit

We do NOT place edging, plantings, statues, candles, or other items outside CFCS policy — so everything we leave stays in place.

Why the Cemetery-Compliant tier exists: Mt Carmel follows strict Archdiocese of Detroit rules to maintain the sacred character of the grounds. Rather than get complicated about what's allowed, we offer this tier as an honest price for exactly what fits those rules — headstone care + approved fresh flowers, nothing more.
