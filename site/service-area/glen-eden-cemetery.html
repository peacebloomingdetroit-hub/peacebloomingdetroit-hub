Grave Cleaning & Decorating at Glen Eden Lutheran Memorial Park (Livonia, MI)

Yes — we clean and decorate graves at Glen Eden Lutheran Memorial Park in Livonia, a well-established non-denominational memorial park serving families across the Livonia and southwest Oakland County area.

Glen Eden Lutheran Memorial Park maintains clear seasonal windows for grave decorations: fresh flowers in approved vases from April 1 through October 31, and winter wreaths and blankets from November 1 through March 1 (removed before the spring cleanup on March 1). The cemetery conducts fall cleanup beginning October 1, so we time our visits to align with these seasonal windows, ensuring your flowers and decorations remain in place.

What we do at Glen Eden:

  • Gentle headstone cleaning and grass trimming
  • Fresh flowers in approved bronze or disposable vases during spring through fall
  • Grave blankets and wreaths (permitted November–March on stands)
  • Flags (12"×18" max) during holiday windows only (Memorial Day, July 4, Flag Day, Veterans Day)
  • Photo proof after every visit

We do NOT: place glass containers, shepherd's hooks, standup decorations, rocks, fencing, hedging, devotional lights, or any items outside approved seasonal windows — we follow the park's maintenance schedule exactly.

Our methods: soft brushes and pH-neutral, stone-safe cleaners only — never bleach or pressure washers, which can damage stone.
