Grave Cleaning & Decorating at Fairview Cemetery (Brighton, MI)

Yes — we clean and decorate graves at Fairview Cemetery in Brighton, one of three municipal cemeteries operated by the City of Brighton.

Fairview Cemetery, along with Old Village Cemetery and Brighton Hills Memorial Gardens, operates under the City of Brighton's rules, which use a permit system for lot decoration and special plantings. We're familiar with the City's requirements and work within them. For specific decoration details, families should obtain a "Decoration of Lots/Special Plantings Permit" from the City Clerk's office, which outlines exactly what's allowed on individual lots.

What we do at Fairview Cemetery:

  • Gentle headstone cleaning and grass trimming around the base
  • Seasonal flowers and approved decorations placed within permit guidelines
  • Flag holders for memorial flag placement (positioned within decorative borders per permit)
  • Photos sent after every visit

Before your first visit: We recommend contacting Brighton City Clerk's office to confirm your lot's specific decoration allowances. Once you know the rules for your plot, we'll follow them exactly.

Our methods: soft brushes and pH-neutral, stone-safe cleaners only — never bleach or pressure washers, which can damage stone.
