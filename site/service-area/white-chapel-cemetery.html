Grave Cleaning & Decorating at White Chapel Memorial Park Cemetery (Troy, MI)

Yes — we clean and decorate graves at White Chapel Memorial Park Cemetery in Troy, a historic and expansive non-denominational memorial park serving families across Metro Detroit since 1928.

White Chapel Memorial Park is a privately owned, 200-acre facility known for its grand architecture, carefully maintained grounds, and commitment to honoring families' wishes for their loved ones' memorials. While the cemetery does not publish a detailed decoration policy online, we work directly with their administration to ensure every service respects their grounds-maintenance standards. Before your first visit, we recommend calling White Chapel at (248) 362–7670 to confirm current decoration guidelines for your specific plot.

What we do at White Chapel:

  • Gentle headstone cleaning and grass trimming around the base
  • Seasonal flower arrangements placed according to cemetery guidelines
  • Photo documentation after every visit
  • We confirm White Chapel's specific rules before every visit, so nothing gets removed

Our methods: soft brushes and pH-neutral, stone-safe cleaners only — never bleach or pressure washers, which can damage stone.

Next step: Call White Chapel at (248) 362–7670 to learn about decoration options for your lot, then contact us to schedule. We work within the cemetery's maintenance standards to honor your loved one.
