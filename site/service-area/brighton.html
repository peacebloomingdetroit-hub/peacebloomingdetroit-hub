Grave Cleaning & Decorating in Brighton, MI

Yes — we clean and decorate graves at municipal cemeteries in the City of Brighton, a Livingston County community just west of our Brighton Township home office.

Cemeteries in Brighton where we serve:

  • Fairview Cemetery
  • St. Patrick's Calvary Cemetery
  • Old Village Cemetery (permit-based — contact City Clerk)
  • Brighton Hills Memorial Gardens (permit-based — contact City Clerk)

Brighton's municipal cemeteries use a permit system for lot decoration and special plantings. We recommend contacting the City of Brighton Clerk's office to confirm your lot's specific allowances, then we'll follow those rules exactly.
