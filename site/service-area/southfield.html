Grave Cleaning & Decorating in Southfield, MI

Yes — we clean and decorate graves at Holy Sepulchre Catholic Cemetery in Southfield, one of the largest Catholic cemeteries in the Archdiocese of Detroit.

Holy Sepulchre follows the same strict Catholic decoration policy as Mt Carmel Cemetery in Wyandotte and Our Lady of Hope Cemetery in Brownstown — all managed by Catholic Funeral & Cemetery Services (CFCS). The cemetery maintains the sacred character of its 350+ acres through careful rules on permitted decorations and seasonal windows. We respect these guidelines completely.

Cemeteries in Southfield where we serve:

  • Holy Sepulchre Catholic Cemetery

Holy Sepulchre serves families across a wide geographic area, and we're experienced with their specific seasonal windows (Easter, Mother's Day, Memorial Day, July 4, Halloween, Christmas, and November vigil lights). Your flowers and tributes will remain in place because they follow the rules.
