Grave Cleaning & Decorating at St. Patrick's Calvary Cemetery (Brighton, MI)

Yes — we clean and decorate graves at St. Patrick's Calvary Cemetery in Brighton, a historic Catholic parish cemetery serving the Brighton community since the 19th century.

St. Patrick's Calvary Cemetery is managed by St. Patrick Catholic Parish. We're happy to serve families there, and we recommend confirming the cemetery's current decoration policy directly with the parish office at (810) 229–9863, extension 212 (cemetery office, Monday–Friday 9 AM–4:30 PM, closed noon on Tuesdays) before scheduling your first visit.

What we do at St. Patrick's Calvary:

  • Gentle headstone cleaning and trimming around the base
  • Seasonal flowers and decorations placed according to the cemetery's policy
  • Photo documentation after every visit
  • We confirm the cemetery's specific rules before every visit, so nothing gets removed

Our methods: soft brushes and pH-neutral, stone-safe cleaners only — never bleach or pressure washers, which can damage stone.

Next step: Call the cemetery office to confirm current decoration guidelines, then contact us to schedule. We work within whatever rules the parish has set.
